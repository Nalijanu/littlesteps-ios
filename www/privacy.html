LittleSteps – Learn & Play Privacy Policy Last updated: 8 November 2026 LittleSteps – Learn & Play is an educational app designed for children aged approximately 3–6. Information We Collect LittleSteps does not collect, transmit, or share personal information. Learning Progress Learning progress and scores are stored locally on the user’s device. This information is not sent to us. Advertising and Analytics LittleSteps does not include third-party advertisements, analytics, or tracking technologies. Children’s Privacy LittleSteps does not require children to create accounts or provide personal information. Data Storage Learning progress is stored on the device. Users can reset their progress within the app. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored progress. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes.
